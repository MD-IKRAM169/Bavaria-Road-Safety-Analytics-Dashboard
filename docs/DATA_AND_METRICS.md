## Metric definitions

Before publishing a dashboard summary, establish the following for **each actual KPI**: exact name, data field(s), formula, filtering rules, denominator, included population, time period, geographic boundaries, null handling and validation test.

Potential dashboard concepts, **not confirmed features**:

- *Incidents by year or month*: specify event-date semantics and whether records represent crashes, people, or vehicles.
- *Severity distribution*: cite the source system's official injury / fatality categories.
- *District comparison*: prefer normalized rates when comparability requires it; document denominator and population coverage.
- *Road-user categories*: document how people and vehicle types are classified and whether categories overlap.

Counts alone cannot establish road-safety risk, causes, or causality. Avoid attributing differences to a particular policy without a controlled study.
